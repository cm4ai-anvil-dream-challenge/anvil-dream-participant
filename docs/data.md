# Data

Teams are encouraged, but not limited to, train on the following resources. The Human Protein Atlas (HPA) provides broad, public coverage of antibody-based protein imaging across many human cell lines and is well suited to training large models across a wide range of proteins and localization patterns. The CM4AI project contributes imaging data from the MDA-MB-468 breast cancer cell line, generated following treatment with DMSO (vehicle control) or one of two cancer drugs, capturing localization shifts driven by drug perturbation.

The challenge held-out test set uses the KOLF2.1J induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC) line, capable of differentiating into multiple cell types, to evaluate whether models generalize beyond the cell states represented in imaging training data. CM4AI has also generated high-throughput molecular data on KOLF2.1J, which may offer teams additional ways to inform predictions for this cell line. Details on these datasets, including data access and availability, are provided on the [Data] subpage.
