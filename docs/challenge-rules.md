# Challenge Rules

---

### Terms and Conditions for Participation

Access to the data requires that you are a registered Synapse user and agree to the following terms and conditions:

- Challenge participants must abide by the guiding principles for responsible research use and data handling within the Synapse platform as required in the Sage Bionetworks [Terms of Service](https://www.synapse.org/TrustCenter:TermsOfService) and by the [DREAM Challenges Official Rules](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn10144147/wiki/448310).

- **Judges' entries:** Individuals in labs that helped organize this challenge, who may have had access to the held-out training/test data, or are in a lab directly funded through the CM4AI AnVIL Generative Image Challenge are not eligible for prize money. Labs affiliated with Bridge2AI or CM4AI but not involved in organizing this specific challenge remain eligible for prizes. Organizer-affiliated teams may still submit methods which will be evaluated and labeled as "judge's entry" or "test-set exposed" on the leaderboard, but will not be eligible for prize consideration or announcement of best performance. To be considered independent submissions, participating groups must attest at registration that neither the group nor any of its members has had, or could reasonably have had, access or exposure to the test set or information derived from it.<br>

- **Prize Eligibility:** For a group of individuals (i.e., team), a designated Team Lead shall be responsible for all communications with Challenge sponsors. To be eligible to receive a cash prize, the Team Lead must be a citizen or permanent resident of the United States. Non-U.S. citizens and non-permanent residents may participate as members of a team that otherwise satisfies the eligibility criteria but are not eligible to win a monetary prize, in whole or in part. For teams that consist of a United States citizen or permanent resident Team Lead but include team members who are non-U.S. citizens and non-permanent residents, the Team Lead shall retain the ability to share the cash prize with such team members after applicable federal and state tax policies have been followed.

- **Data Use:** Use of the data must comply with the use and citation policies of all data sources.

- **Acknowledgement and Citation:** Registered Challenge participants are permitted to use, publish and present their Challenge Results after the publication embargo period concludes, provided they acknowledge the CM4AI Dream Challenge and all cite data sources in accordance with their individual citation policies as summarized below.

- **Embargo**: Challenge participants agree not to report overall Challenge results or any analysis related to the overall results in this Challenge until the organizers and Challenge participants have jointly published an overview paper on the results from the DREAM Challenge and the best performing strategies used in the Challenge.

- You agree not to use the Challenge data, either alone or in concert with any other information, to make any effort to identify or contact individuals who are the subjects of the data.

- You agree to use the Challenge data in compliance with all applicable laws, rules, and regulations, as well as all applicable professional standards.

- You confirm that if you inadvertently receive identifiable information (other than age >89) or otherwise identify a subject, you will promptly notify the Privacy and Compliance Office (PCO) by emailing pco@sagebase.org.

### Communication Policy

The Discussion forum will be the primary means of informal discussion, Q&A, and other interactions between the Challenge members (organizers, participants, etc).  With the exception of sensitive topics, we **request that all Challenge-related questions be posed here rather than through private discussion**.  We cannot guarantee that emails directly sent to members of the Organizers team will be given priority in getting answered.

When interacting on the discussion forum:

* Informal language is encouraged in the forums, but remember that everything you say is public.  Please be courteous and avoid using disrespectful language -- we want to keep the forums a place of positive support for everyone.<br>

* As organizers, we will do our best in responding to all questions posted between Monday 9AM to Friday 5PM Pacific Time within 24 hours, whereas questions posted outside of the business week (including holidays), will be answered on the following business day.
